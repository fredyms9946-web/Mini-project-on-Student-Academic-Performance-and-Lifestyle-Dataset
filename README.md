#Mini Project-
This project explores how students' lifestyle habits influence their academic performance. It analyses factors such as study hours, sleep duration, gaming time, social media usage, and other daily activities to understand their relationship with students' academic outcomes.

Objectives

Analyse the relationship between lifestyle habits and academic performance.

Identify factors that positively or negatively influence students' grades.

Explore the impact of study hours, sleep, gaming, and social media.

Present meaningful insights through data visualisation.
